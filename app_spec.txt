<project_specification>
  <project_name>Elevate CRM</project_name>

  <overview>
    A full-scale, multi-tenant Sales and Marketing CRM platform that replaces GHL and HubSpot. Built for Elevate clients, the platform enables sales teams and marketing teams to run their complete workflows without leaving the app. Features include Elev8 Playbook-driven pipelines, universal lead scoring, AI-powered landing page builder, unified communications inbox, automation engine, and comprehensive integrations.
  </overview>
